# Returning to work – Surrogacy rights at work

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/leave-and-pay-when-you-have-a-child-through-surrogacy/returning-to-work_

There are some things you should consider when you go back to work after adoption leave.

## Telling your employer when you'll return

You do not need to tell your employer when you'll return to work. But if you know the date you want to return, you can let your employer know before you leave. This will help your employer plan for your return.

## If you do not tell your employer about a return date

If you're not sure when you want to return or you do not give your employer a return date, they'll assume you'll return to work after 52 weeks.

## Changing the date you return

If you want to return to work early, you must give your employer 8 weeks' notice.

It might be possible to agree a return date with less than 8 weeks' notice but only if your employer agrees.

## Not returning to work

If you do not want to return to work after your adoption leave, you must give your employer notice. Check your employment contract to find out how much notice you must give.

## Return to work rights

Your right to return to the same job as before depends on how much adoption leave you've taken.

### If you've taken adoption leave of up to 26 weeks

You have the right to return to the same job.

### If you've taken adoption leave of more than 26 weeks

You have the right to return to the same job unless your employer has a good reason to offer you another one. For example, if the job no longer exists or there have been changes to the organisation.

In this case, your employer can offer you a similar job instead. The job must be suitable and appropriate.

It cannot be worse than the previous job. For example, it must have the same or similar:

- pay
- benefits
- holiday entitlement
- seniority
- location

### If you think your employer is not being fair

Your employer should not dismiss you or treat you unfairly just because you're taking adoption leave.

There is extra redundancy protection for employees who take adoption leave. [Find out about redundancy protection for pregnancy and new parents](https://www.acas.org.uk/redundancy-protection-for-pregnancy-and-new-parents "Redundancy protection for pregnancy and new parents").

## If you want to change your hours or job

If you want to change your hours or duties when you return to work, you might be able to make a flexible working request.

[Find out how to make a flexible working request](https://www.acas.org.uk/statutory-flexible-working-requests "Statutory flexible working requests")
