# Shared parental leave templates for parents

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/shared-parental-leave-form-templates_

Forms and templates for eligible parents who want to take shared parental leave. Free to download and use.

Use these templates alongside our advice on [shared parental leave](https://www.acas.org.uk/shared-parental-leave-and-pay "Shared parental leave and pay").

For more advice, you can also, [contact the Acas helpline](https://www.acas.org.uk/contact "Contact us").

If you need the forms in a different format, or you cannot download them, email [digital@acas.org.uk](mailto:digital@acas.org.uk)

## Maternity

'Notice of entitlement' forms. For either parent to tell their employer they're entitled to and intend to take shared parental leave. To use when the mother or birth parent will end maternity leave early.
