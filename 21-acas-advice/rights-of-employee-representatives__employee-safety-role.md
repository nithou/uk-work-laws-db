# Employee safety role – Rights of employee representatives

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/rights-of-employee-representatives/employee-safety-role_

Representatives of employee safety are non-union employee representatives.

These representatives can be appointed in organisations where:

- there's no recognised trade union
- there's a recognised trade union but they have not appointed a health and safety representative
- groups of workers are outside the scope of trade union recognition – for example, workers who are not union members

The trade union equivalent of this role is a health and safety representative.

You can also read our advice on the [rights of trade union representatives](https://www.acas.org.uk/rights-of-trade-union-representatives "Rights of trade union representatives").

## Legal rights

A representative of employee safety has a legal right to:

- paid time off for certain duties
- paid time off for relevant training
- access to facilities to carry out certain duties
- protection from dismissal and detriment – this applies to carrying out representative duties and activities

The main law that covers this is the Health and Safety (Consultation with Employees) Regulations 1996.
