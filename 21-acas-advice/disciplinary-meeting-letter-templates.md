# Disciplinary hearing letter templates

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-meeting-letter-templates_

Templates for employers and managers to arrange a disciplinary hearing. Free to download and use.

A hearing is a meeting where the employer hears all the evidence before making a decision.

[Find out more about disciplinary and grievance procedures](https://www.acas.org.uk/discipline-and-grievance "Discipline and grievance")

## Where a warning is being considered

A letter giving notice of a disciplinary hearing where a first or final written warning is being considered.

[Notice of disciplinary hearing letter: possible warning](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-meeting-letter-templates/disciplinary-meeting-letter-for-warning-template "Notice of disciplinary hearing letter: possible warning")

## Where dismissal is being considered

A letter giving notice of a disciplinary hearing where dismissal or other serious action is being considered.

[Notice of disciplinary hearing letter: possible dismissal](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-meeting-letter-templates/disciplinary-meeting-letter-for-possible-dismissal-template "Notice of disciplinary hearing letter: possible dismissal")
