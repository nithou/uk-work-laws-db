# What the content strategy is – Content strategy for the web

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/content-strategy-for-the-web_

The content strategy will explain:

- briefly, the web's role in Acas's services
- areas of focus for improving the web channel across services

## Why it's needed

Acas has 4 Digital product teams. Each has responsibility for part of Acas's web presence.

The content strategy for the web will help product teams:

- understand each other's use of the web
- deliver improvements across the channel
- coordinate content work

It will support the teams to deliver Acas's organisational ambitions and aims. These include:

- the 3 ambitions of the [Acas strategy 2025 to 2030](https://www.acas.org.uk/about-us/acas-strategy-2025-to-2030 "Acas strategy 2025 to 2030")
- the 3 aims of the communications and external affairs strategy
