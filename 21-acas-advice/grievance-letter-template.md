# Grievance letter template

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/grievance-letter-template_

A template letter for workers to use to raise a formal grievance with their employer. Free to use.

You can copy and paste the template text into an email or document.

[Find out more about formal grievance procedures](https://www.acas.org.uk/grievance-procedure-step-by-step "Formal grievance procedure")

## Template

**[Today's date]**

Dear **[name of employer / HR manager / line manager]**,

I am writing to raise a formal grievance.

I have a **[problem with / complaint about]** **[give details]**.

**[Optional]** I have evidence in the form of **[give details]**.

**[Optional]** I would like **[give details of what you want your employer to do to resolve the grievance]**.

Please let me know when I can meet you to talk about my grievance.

I would like to be accompanied at the meeting by **[name]**.

Yours sincerely,

**[Your name]**
