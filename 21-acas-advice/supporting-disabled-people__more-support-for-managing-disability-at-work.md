# Specialist help and support – Supporting disabled people at work

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/supporting-disabled-people/more-support-for-managing-disability-at-work_

Specialist help and support is available for anyone who's managing a disability at work or supporting others.

## Employment law or workplace advice

For any work-related problems or questions around disability, employees and employers can:

- [contact the Acas helpline](https://www.acas.org.uk/contact "Contact us")
- [get legal advice](https://www.acas.org.uk/getting-legal-advice "Getting legal advice")
- talk to your trade union or employers' association if you're a member

### For employees

If you're disabled and think you're being discriminated against or unfairly dismissed, you can:

- [contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS)](https://www.equalityadvisoryservice.com/)

If you're thinking about leaving work:

- [read Scope's advice on stopping work or taking ill health retirement](https://www.scope.org.uk/advice-and-support/stopping-work-ill-health-retirement/)

### For employers

Employers can:

- [find support with employee health and disability on GOV.UK](https://www.support-with-employee-health-and-disability.dwp.gov.uk/support-with-employee-health-and-disability)
- [read the Health and Safety Executive's guidance on supporting disabled workers](https://www.hse.gov.uk/disability/best-practice/overview.htm)

## Access to Work

Access to Work is a government scheme that can help people with a physical or mental health condition or disability to get or stay in work. Someone might be eligible for:

- a grant to help pay for practical support with work
- advice about managing mental health at work

[Find out more about Access to Work](https://www.gov.uk/access-to-work)

## Business Disability Forum

Business Disability Forum is a not for profit membership organisation that helps employers support disabled staff and customers.

[Find out more about Business Disability Forum](https://businessdisabilityforum.org.uk)

## Disability Confident employer scheme

Disability Confident is a government scheme to help employers recruit, retain and develop disabled people.

[Find out more about the Disability Confident employer scheme](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/disability-confident-campaign)

## Support for specific disabilities

[Help with work from Macmillan Cancer](https://www.macmillan.org.uk/cancer-information-and-support/get-help/help-with-work) – for people who have or had cancer and employers

[Learning disability employment services from Mencap](https://www.mencap.org.uk/advice-and-support/employment-services) – for people with a learning disability and employers

[Mental health support from Mind](http://www.mind.org.uk) – advice and support, including a legal advice line

[Advice on specific conditions on GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/employing-disabled-people-and-people-with-health-conditions/employing-disabled-people-and-people-with-health-conditions#advice-on-specific-conditions) – advice and support for a wide range of health conditions and disabilities

## Emotional support

Some employers offer support through work, for example:

- counselling or mental health support through an employee assistance programme (EAP)
- staff support networks – for example a disability network
- a mentoring scheme to support disabled people

If you need someone else to talk to, you can contact:

- [Samaritans](https://www.samaritans.org/how-we-can-help/contact-samaritan)
- [Scope](https://www.scope.org.uk)
- [Disability Information Scotland](https://www.disabilityscot.org.uk)

## Training and tailored support for employers

Acas offers [tailored support for employers](https://www.acas.org.uk/tailored-support "Tailored support for your organisation") to address specific challenges in an organisation.

Our training for employers and managers includes:

- [reasonable adjustments training](https://obs.acas.org.uk/EventsList.aspx?SubRegionId=-1&SearchTopicId=121&SubRegion=--%20All%20Regions%20--&SearchTopic=Reasonable%20adjustments)
- [equality, diversity and inclusion training](https://obs.acas.org.uk/EventsList.aspx?SubRegionId=-1&SearchTopicId=22&SubRegion=--%20All%20Regions%20--&SearchTopic=Diversity%20in%20the%20workplace)
- [mental health in the workplace training](https://obs.acas.org.uk/EventsList.aspx?SubRegionId=-1&SearchTopicId=84&SubRegion=--%20All%20Regions%20--&SearchTopic=Mental%20health%20in%20the%20workplace)
