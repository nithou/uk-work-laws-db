# Disciplinary outcome letter templates

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-outcome-letter-templates_

Disciplinary outcome letter templates for employers and managers. To use after following a full disciplinary procedure to decide on an outcome. Free to download and use.

[Find out more about disciplinary and grievance procedures](https://www.acas.org.uk/discipline-and-grievance "Discipline and grievance")

If you need these letters in a different format, or you cannot download them, email [digital@acas.org.uk](mailto:digital@acas.org.uk)

## Where the outcome is a warning

A letter to an employee giving a disciplinary outcome of a first or final written warning.

[Notice of disciplinary outcome letter: warning](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-outcome-letter-templates/notice-of-disciplinary-outcome-letter-template-warning "Notice of disciplinary outcome letter: warning")

## Where the outcome is dismissal, disciplinary action or no action

A letter to an employee giving the disciplinary outcome of dismissal, disciplinary action or no action.

[Notice of disciplinary outcome letter: dismissal, disciplinary action or no action](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-outcome-letter-templates/notice-of-disciplinary-outcome-letter-template-dismissal-disciplinary-action-or-no-action "Notice of disciplinary outcome letter: dismissal, disciplinary action or no action")
