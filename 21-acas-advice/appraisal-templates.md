# Appraisal form templates

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/appraisal-templates_

Templates for employers to review and record employees' performance. Free to download and use.

If you need the templates in a different format, or you cannot download it, email [digital@acas.org.uk](mailto:digital@acas.org.uk)
