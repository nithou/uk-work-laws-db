# Ethnicity pay gap reporting

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/ethnicity-pay-gap-reporting_

The 'ethnicity pay gap' is the difference in average earnings between all ethnic groups. While it's not a legal requirement, many organisations already report on their ethnicity pay gap.

[Follow the ethnicity pay gap reporting guidance on GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ethnicity-pay-reporting-guidance-for-employers)
