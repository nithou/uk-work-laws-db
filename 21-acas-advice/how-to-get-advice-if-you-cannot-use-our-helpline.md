# How to get advice if you cannot use our helpline

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/how-to-get-advice-if-you-cannot-use-our-helpline_

The quickest way to get advice from us is by [calling the Acas helpline](https://www.acas.org.uk/contact "Contact us"). During a call, an adviser can discuss your situation to give you the most relevant advice.

If you have an accessibility need that means you cannot call the Acas helpline, you can use our form to ask for advice in another way.

For example, if you:

- find it difficult to speak on the phone
- need a British Sign Language (BSL) interpreter
- are not in the UK
- do not have enough phone credit to call

We can respond either by:

- email – usually within 5 working days
- British Sign Language (BSL) or lip reading video call – usually within 2 working days
- phone – usually within 2 working days

Use our form to tell us about your situation and what advice you need from us.

Our advice is free and confidential. To find out how we use, store and protect your personal information, read our [privacy policy](https://www.acas.org.uk/privacy "Privacy notice").
