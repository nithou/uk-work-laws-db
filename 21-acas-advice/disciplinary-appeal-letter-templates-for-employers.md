# Disciplinary appeal letter templates for employers

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-appeal-letter-templates-for-employers_

Templates for employers to arrange disciplinary appeal meetings or give their employee the result of the appeal. Free to download and use.

[Find out more about disciplinary procedures](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-procedure-step-by-step "Disciplinary procedure")

If you need the letter in a different format, or you cannot download it, email [digital@acas.org.uk](mailto:digital@acas.org.uk)

## When an employee has appealed against a warning

A template letter for an employer to acknowledge their employee's appeal against a written warning and to arrange an appeal meeting.

[Notice of appeal meeting against a warning](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-appeal-letter-templates-for-employers/notice-of-appeal-meeting-against-warning "Notice of appeal meeting against warning")

## Giving the outcome of an appeal against a warning

A template letter for an employer to give their employee the final decision of an appeal against a written warning.

[Notice of result of appeal against a warning](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-appeal-letter-templates-for-employers/notice-of-result-of-appeal-against-a-warning "Notice of result of appeal against a warning ")

## When an employee has appealed against a dismissal

A template letter for an employer to acknowledge their employee's appeal against a dismissal or other serious disciplinary action and to arrange an appeal meeting.

[Notice of appeal meeting against a dismissal or serious disciplinary action](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-appeal-letter-templates-for-employers/notice-of-appeal-meeting-against-a-dismissal-or-serious-disciplinary-action "Notice of appeal meeting against a dismissal or serious disciplinary action ")

## Giving the outcome of an appeal against a dismissal

A template letter for an employer to give an employee the final decision of an appeal against a dismissal or other serious disciplinary action.

[Notice of appeal result against a dismissal or other serious disciplinary action](https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-appeal-letter-templates-for-employers/notice-of-appeal-result-against-a-dismissal-or-other-serious-disciplinary-action "Notice of appeal result against a dismissal or other serious disciplinary action ")
