# Becoming the child's legal parent – Surrogacy rights at work

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/leave-and-pay-when-you-have-a-child-through-surrogacy_

Surrogacy is when someone else carries and gives birth to a baby for the intended parents.

If you use a surrogate, the surrogate will be the child's legal parent at birth.

You must apply to become the legal parent within 6 months of the child's birth to get adoption rights, leave and pay.

You must apply for:

- a parental order – if one intended parent is genetically related to the child
- an adoption order – if the intended parents are not genetically related to the child

If you apply for an adoption order, the rules for adoption are different.

[Find out your adoption leave, pay and other rights](https://www.acas.org.uk/leave-and-pay-when-you-have-a-child-through-surrogacy/adoption-leave-and-pay "Adoption leave and pay")
