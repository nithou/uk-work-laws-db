# Employee – Employment status

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/employment-status/employee-employment-status_

People with 'employee' employment status have:

- more employment rights than workers or self-employed people
- more obligations towards their employer

## What makes someone an employee

Someone is likely to have the legal status of employee if most of these things apply:

- they're required to work regularly unless they're on leave
- they can usually expect work to be consistently available
- they cannot unreasonably refuse to do the work
- they get statutory paid holiday – they might also have additional contractual holiday entitlement
- they're subject to the employer's discipline and grievance procedures
- they need to give notice to their employer if they want to take maternity, paternity or adoption leave
- they cannot get someone else to do their job
- their employer decides how, when and where they do their work
- their employer provides the materials, tools and equipment for their work

If most of these do not apply, someone is more likely to be a [worker](https://www.acas.org.uk/employment-status/worker-employment-status "Worker") or [self-employed](https://www.acas.org.uk/employment-status/self-employment "Self-employment").

## Employment rights

Employees' rights include:

- a [written statement of employment particulars](https://www.acas.org.uk/what-must-be-written-in-an-employment-contract "Written statements") outlining their job rights and responsibilities
- [minimum wage](https://www.acas.org.uk/national-minimum-wage-entitlement "National Minimum Wage")
- [paid holiday](https://www.acas.org.uk/checking-holiday-entitlement "Holiday entitlement")
- [payslips](https://www.acas.org.uk/payslips "Payslips")
- protection for [whistleblowing](https://www.acas.org.uk/whistleblowing-at-work "Whistleblowing at work")
- protection against [discrimination](https://www.acas.org.uk/discrimination-and-the-law "Discrimination at work")
- protection from [less favourable treatment for working part time](https://www.acas.org.uk/part-time-workers/if-someone-who-works-part-time-is-treated-less-favourably "If someone's treated less favourably")
- making [statutory flexible working requests](https://www.acas.org.uk/statutory-flexible-working-requests "Statutory flexible working requests")

As long as they meet any eligibility criteria, they're also entitled to:

- [statutory sick pay (SSP)](https://www.acas.org.uk/checking-sick-pay/statutory-sick-pay-ssp "Statutory sick pay")
- [ordinary parental leave](https://www.acas.org.uk/parental-leave "Ordinary parental leave")
- [shared parental leave and pay](https://www.acas.org.uk/shared-parental-leave-and-pay "Shared parental leave and pay")
- [maternity leave and pay](https://www.acas.org.uk/statutory-maternity-leave-and-pay "Statutory maternity leave and pay")
- [paternity leave and pay](https://www.acas.org.uk/paternity-rights-leave-and-pay "Paternity leave and pay")
- [adoption leave and pay](https://www.acas.org.uk/your-adoption-leave-pay-and-other-rights "Adoption leave and pay")
- [parental bereavement leave and pay](https://www.acas.org.uk/parental-bereavement-leave-and-pay "Parental bereavement leave and pay")
- [neonatal care leave and pay](https://www.acas.org.uk/neonatal-care-leave-and-pay "Neonatal care leave and pay")
- [carer's leave](https://www.acas.org.uk/carers-leave "Carer's leave")
- [time off for dependants](https://www.acas.org.uk/time-off-for-dependants "Time off for dependants")
- [bereaved partner's paternity leave](https://www.acas.org.uk/bereaved-partners-paternity-leave "Bereaved partner's paternity leave")
- time off for public duties
- [redundancy pay](https://www.acas.org.uk/your-rights-during-redundancy/redundancy-pay "Redundancy pay") after 2 years' continuous service, if their role becomes redundant
- claim [unfair dismissal or automatically unfair dismissal](https://www.acas.org.uk/dismissals/unfair-dismissal "Unfair dismissal")
- get the minimum [notice period](https://www.acas.org.uk/notice-periods "Notice periods") if they're dismissed or made redundant
- [protection against dismissal or detriment for taking action over a health and safety issue](https://www.acas.org.uk/health-and-safety-at-work/protection-from-dismissal-and-detriment "Protection from dismissal and detriment")
