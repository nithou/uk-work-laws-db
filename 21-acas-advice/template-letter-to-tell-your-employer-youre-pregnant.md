# Template letter to tell your employer you're pregnant

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/template-letter-to-tell-your-employer-youre-pregnant_

A template letter to tell your employer you're pregnant. Free to use.

Anyone with the [legal status of employee](https://www.acas.org.uk/employment-status/employee-employment-status "Employee") is entitled to maternity leave.

Someone is not likely to be an employee if they're:

- an agency worker
- a casual worker
- on a zero-hours contract

If you're entitled to statutory maternity leave, you must tell your employer you're pregnant. You need to tell them no later than the 15th week before your baby is due or as soon as possible if this is not reasonably practical.

If you're entitled to statutory maternity pay you must give your employer notice 28 days before you expect pay to start or as soon as possible if this is not reasonably practical.

You can copy and paste the template text into an email or document.

[Find advice on planning leave](https://www.acas.org.uk/shared-parental-leave-and-pay/taking-shared-parental-leave "Planning leave")

## Template

**[Today's date]**

Dear **[name of your employer / HR manager / line manager]**,

I am writing to tell you that I am pregnant.

My baby is due on **[date]**.

I would like to start my maternity leave on **[date]**.

Yours sincerely,

**[Your name]**
