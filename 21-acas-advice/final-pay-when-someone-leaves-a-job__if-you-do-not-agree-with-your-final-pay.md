# If you do not agree with your pay – Final pay when someone leaves a job

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/final-pay-when-someone-leaves-a-job/if-you-do-not-agree-with-your-final-pay_

If you think your final pay is wrong, you should raise this with your employer. It's usually best to raise the problem informally first. [Find out more about raising a problem at work](https://www.acas.org.uk/how-to-raise-a-problem-at-work "Talking with your employer about a problem").

If you've tried all options to resolve the issue, you might be able to make a claim to an [employment tribunal](https://www.acas.org.uk/employment-tribunals "Employment tribunals").
