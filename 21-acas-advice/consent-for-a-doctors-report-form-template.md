# Consent for a doctor's report form template

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/consent-for-a-doctors-report-form-template_

Template for a consent form for an employer to ask for a worker's permission to approach their doctor for health information. Free to use.

You must get permission from the worker before contacting their doctor.

Use this template:

- alongside our [advice on getting a doctor's report about a worker's health](https://www.acas.org.uk/getting-a-doctors-report-about-an-employees-health "Getting a doctor's report about a worker's health")
- as an attachment to our [template letter to a worker asking for permission for a doctor's report](https://www.acas.org.uk/template-letter-to-a-worker-asking-for-permission-for-a-doctors-report "Letter to a worker asking for permission for a doctor's report")

You can copy and paste the template text into an email or document.

## Template text – consent for a doctor's report form

Name:

Address:

Date of birth:

Doctor name:

Doctor address:

I give consent for my employer to get a medical report from my doctor.

☐ Yes

☐ No

I want to see a copy of the report before it is sent to my employer.

☐ Yes

☐ No

I confirm that if I do want to see a copy of the report, I will contact the doctor within 21 days of my employer requesting it.

I confirm I have read and understood my rights under the Medical Reports Act 1988.

☐ Yes

☐ No

Signed:
