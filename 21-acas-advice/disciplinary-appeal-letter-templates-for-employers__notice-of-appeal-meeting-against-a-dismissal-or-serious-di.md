# Notice of appeal meeting against a dismissal or serious disciplinary action

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-appeal-letter-templates-for-employers/notice-of-appeal-meeting-against-a-dismissal-or-serious-disciplinary-action_

A template letter for an employer to acknowledge their employee's appeal against a dismissal or other serious disciplinary action and to arrange an appeal meeting. Free to use.

You can copy and paste the template text into an email or document.

## Template

**[Today's date]**

Dear **[name of employee]**

You have appealed against your **[dismissal / disciplinary action]** on **[date]**, which was confirmed to you in writing on **[date]**.

Your appeal will be heard by **[panel or employee name]** in **[location or building]** on **[date]** at **[time]**.

By law, you have the right to be accompanied by a 'companion'. This can be:

- someone you work with
- a trade union representative who's certified by their union to act as a companion
- an official employed by a trade union

If you would like to be accompanied, tell me the name of the person who will be coming with you **[number of hours or days]** before the hearing.

The decision of this appeal meeting is final and there is no further right of review.

Yours sincerely,

**[Signature and full name of manager / employer]**
