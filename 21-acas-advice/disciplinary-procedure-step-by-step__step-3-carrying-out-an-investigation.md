# Step 3: Carrying out an investigation – Disciplinary procedure

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/disciplinary-procedure-step-by-step/step-3-carrying-out-an-investigation_

To follow a fair procedure, the employer must carry out an investigation. This is to get as much information as they reasonably can about their employee's alleged misconduct or poor performance.
