# Shared parental leave letter templates for employers

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/shared-parental-leave-forms-and-templates-for-employers_

Templates for employers to confirm shared parental leave for eligible parents. Free to download and use.

Use these templates alongside our advice on [shared parental leave](https://www.acas.org.uk/shared-parental-leave-and-pay "Shared parental leave and pay").

If you need the letters in a different format, or you cannot download them, email [digital@acas.org.uk](mailto:digital@acas.org.uk)

## Confirming entitlement

For an employer to confirm they have received an employee's 'notice of entitlement' to shared parental leave.

[Letter confirming entitlement to shared parental leave (Word version, 15KB)](https://www.acas.org.uk/sites/default/files/2026-07/Confirmation-letter-of-entitlement-to-shared-parental-leave.docx)

[Letter confirming entitlement to shared parental leave (OpenDocument Text format, 7KB)](https://www.acas.org.uk/sites/default/files/2026-07/Confirmation-letter-of-entitlement-to-shared-parental-leave.odt)

## Confirming booking

For an employer to confirm an employee's shared parental leave booking.

[Letter confirming booking of shared parental leave (Word version, 14KB)](https://www.acas.org.uk/sites/default/files/2026-08/Confirmation-letter-of-shared-parental-leave-booking.docx)

[Letter confirming booking of shared parental leave (OpenDocument Text format, 6KB)](https://www.acas.org.uk/sites/default/files/2026-08/Confirmation-letter-of-shared-parental-leave-booking.odt)

For an employer to refuse a request for discontinuous shared parental leave.
