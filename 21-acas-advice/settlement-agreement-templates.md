# Settlement agreement templates

_Source: Acas — https://www.acas.org.uk/settlement-agreement-templates_

Templates for discussing and making a settlement agreement. Free to download and use.

A settlement agreement can be used by both sides in a dispute to resolve a problem or end employment. You should also follow the [Acas Code of Practice on settlement agreements](https://www.acas.org.uk/acas-code-of-practice-settlement-agreements "Acas Code of Practice on settlement agreements").

[Read our advice on settlement agreements](https://www.acas.org.uk/settlement-agreements "Settlement agreements")
