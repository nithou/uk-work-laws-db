# Time limits — check these first

## The October 2026 change

The Employment Rights Act 2025, Schedule 12, and the Employment Tribunal (Extension of Time Limits) (Miscellaneous Amendments and Transitional Provisions) Regulations 2026 extend most employment tribunal time limits from **3 months to 6 months**. The longer limit applies where the **relevant date** (usually the date of the act complained of, the last act in a series, or the effective date of termination) is **on or after 1 October 2026** (SI 2026/954, reg. 4 and Schedule). Where the relevant date is **before 1 October 2026**, the old 3-month limit applies (subject to early conciliation extensions).

Scotland: breach-of-contract claims in the tribunal depend on a separate Scottish extension-of-jurisdiction order (expected to change from 9 November 2026 — check).

## Main limits (relevant date on or after 1 October 2026)

| Claim | Time limit | Runs from | Extension test | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unfair dismissal | 6 months | Effective date of termination (EDT) | "Not reasonably practicable" (strict) | ERA s.111 |
| Discrimination, harassment, victimisation (Equality Act) | 6 months | Act complained of; conduct extending over a period = end of the period | "Just and equitable" (broader) | EqA s.123 |
| Unlawful deduction from wages (incl. holiday pay, NMW shortfall claimed as deductions) | 6 months | Date of payment of the deduction, or the last in a series | Not reasonably practicable; 2-year backstop on how far back deductions can be claimed (s.23(4A)) | ERA s.23 |
| Whistleblowing detriment | 6 months | Act or failure (last in a series) | Not reasonably practicable | ERA s.48 |
| Redundancy payment | 6 months to make a written claim to the employer or bring a tribunal claim (extendable to 12 months if just and equitable) | Relevant date | — | ERA s.164 |
| Collective consultation failure (protective award) | 6 months | Date of the last dismissal | Not reasonably practicable | TULRCA s.189(5) |
| Breach of contract (wrongful dismissal, unpaid notice, contractual sums outstanding on termination) — England & Wales | 6 months | EDT | Not reasonably practicable | ET Extension of Jurisdiction Order 1994 as amended 2026 |
| Working time / holiday (WTR reg. 30) | 6 months | Date the right should have been allowed or the payment made | Not reasonably practicable | WTR reg. 30 |
| Trade union detriment, time off for union duties and activities | 6 months | Act or failure | Not reasonably practicable | TULRCA ss.147, 171 |
| Equal pay (sex equality clause) | 6 months **after the end of the employment** (stable employment situation rules) | End of employment | Limited extensions (concealment, incapacity) | EqA s.129 |
| Flexible working request procedure | 6 months | Date of the breach / decision | — | ERA s.80H |

For relevant dates **before 1 October 2026**, read "3 months" in place of "6 months" (equal pay: 6 months unchanged; redundancy payment: 6 months unchanged).

## Acas early conciliation (EC) — mandatory first step

- Before presenting most claims, the prospective claimant must notify Acas (online form or telephone) and obtain an **EC certificate** with a unique reference number (Employment Tribunals Act 1996 s.18A; Early Conciliation Rules 2014).
- Since 1 December 2025 the conciliation period is **up to 12 weeks** from Acas receiving the notification (Rules, Schedule para. 6).
- **Stop-the-clock**: the days from the day after Acas is contacted (Day A) to the day the certificate is received (Day B) do not count; and if the limit would otherwise expire during the period from Day A to one month after Day B, it is extended to one month after Day B (ERA s.207B; EqA s.140B). Contacting Acas **after** the original limit has expired does not revive it.
- One EC certificate per matter and per respondent; name every potential respondent (including individual harassers if relevant).
- Exemptions are narrow (e.g. interim relief claims in whistleblowing or trade union dismissals, multiple claimants where another has a certificate).

## Practical rules

1. Count from the **act** or **EDT**, not from the outcome of an internal grievance or appeal. Pursuing an appeal does not delay the EDT.
2. Diary the deadline on the day of the act; contact Acas well before it.
3. After the certificate, present the **ET1** quickly — ideally well within the month.
4. For a continuing act or series (discrimination, deductions), identify the **last** act; earlier acts may be out of time if they are not part of the same series or conduct extending over a period.
5. Late claims: "not reasonably practicable" is strict (ignorance of rights is rarely enough; reliance on wrong advice from a skilled adviser usually fails — Dedman v British Building [1974]); "just and equitable" is more flexible (Abertawe Bro Morgannwg UHB v Morgan [2018] EWCA Civ 640) but still not automatic.
6. Interim relief (automatically unfair dismissal for whistleblowing or trade union reasons): application within **7 days** of the EDT (ERA s.128; TULRCA s.161).
