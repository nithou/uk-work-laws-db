# Worked calculations

## 1. "A week's pay" (ERA ss.220–229)

- Normal working hours and pay that does not vary: gross weekly pay under the contract at the calculation date (s.221).
- Pay varying with the amount of work (commission, piecework) or no normal working hours: average over the **12 weeks** ending with the calculation date (ss.221(3), 224).
- For statutory redundancy pay, the basic award and other statutory payments: capped at **£751** (s.227, from 6 April 2026; £719 before).
- For the compensatory award, the 52-week cap uses the actual (uncapped) gross week's pay.
- Holiday pay uses its own 52-week reference period (WTR reg. 16).

## 2. Statutory minimum notice (ERA s.86)

| Continuous employment | Employer must give at least |
|---|---|
| 1 month to < 2 years | 1 week |
| 2 to < 12 years | 1 week per complete year |
| 12 years or more | 12 weeks |

Contractual notice applies if longer. Employee's minimum: 1 week.

## 3. Statutory redundancy pay / basic award

Count complete years of continuous service backwards from the relevant date (max 20), with:
- age 41+ in that year: 1.5 weeks
- age 22–40: 1 week
- under 22: 0.5 week

**Example**: age 45, 12 years' service, gross pay £900/week (capped to £751).
Years at 41–45 (5 years) × 1.5 = 7.5; years at ages 33–40 (7 years) × 1 = 7 → **14.5 weeks × £751 = £10,889.50**.

Maximum: 20 years × 1.5 × £751 = **£22,530**.

The **basic award** for unfair dismissal uses the same formula (s.119), with service ending at the EDT; reduced by any redundancy payment already made; minimum £9,157 for certain automatically unfair reasons (s.120).

## 4. Compensatory award (sketch)

1. **Immediate loss**: net pay and benefits from the EDT to the hearing, minus earnings from new work and notice pay received.
2. **Future loss**: net weekly loss × realistic number of weeks to find equivalent work (or until the loss ends).
3. **Pension loss**: employer contributions (defined contribution) or Presidential Guidance method (defined benefit).
4. **Loss of statutory rights**: conventional sum (often £500–£1,000).
5. Deduct: payments already made (ex gratia sums beyond statutory redundancy pay may be credited).
6. Adjust: **Polkey** %, **Acas uplift/reduction** (±25%), **contributory fault**, Employment Act 2002 s.38 award.
7. Apply the **cap** (lower of £123,543 or 52 weeks' gross pay) — only if the EDT is **before 1 January 2027**; no cap for whistleblowing or health and safety dismissals, and no cap at all for EDTs from 1 January 2027.
8. Grossing up for tax if the total exceeds £30,000.

## 5. Holiday entitlement and pay

- Standard: 5.6 weeks × days worked per week (max 28 days). Part-time 3 days/week → 16.8 days.
- Leaving mid-year: accrued = (5.6 × weekly days) × (portion of leave year worked) − days taken; daily rate for payment in lieu: a day's pay (often 1/260 of annual salary for a 5-day week; contract may specify).
- Irregular-hours/part-year worker: 12.07% × hours worked in the pay period. Example: 120 hours in a month → 14.48 hours of holiday accrued.
- Variable pay: average weekly remuneration over the last 52 paid weeks (including regular overtime and commission for the 4-week portion).

## 6. SSP (from 6 April 2026)

Weekly SSP = lower of £123.25 or 80% of average weekly earnings, paid per qualifying day from day one.
**Example**: average weekly earnings £140 → 80% = £112 → SSP £112/week; for a 5-day week, £22.40 per qualifying day.
**Example**: earnings £400/week → 80% = £320 → capped at £123.25/week.

## 7. Protective award (collective redundancy)

Up to 180 days' **gross** pay per affected employee (dismissals on or after 6 April 2026; previously 90 days), no cap on week's pay. Example: £600/week, maximum award = 180/7 × £600 ≈ **£15,428.57**.

## 8. Injury to feelings (discrimination)

Vento bands for claims presented on or after 6 April 2026: lower £1,300–£12,600; middle £12,600–£37,700; upper £37,700–£62,900. Interest at 8% (England & Wales) from the date of the act.

## 9. National minimum wage check

Total remuneration in the pay reference period (minus excluded payments and reductions) ÷ hours of working time in that period ≥ applicable rate. Example: monthly pay £2,000 for 165 hours → £12.12/hour < £12.71 → underpayment £0.59 × 165 = £97.35 for the period (arrears are paid at the current rate).
