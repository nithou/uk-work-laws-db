# Calculate your employee's Statutory Parental Bereavement Pay

_Source: GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk/calculate-statutory-parental-bereavement-pay — updated 2026-04-06_

Use this tool to calculate your employee’s Statutory Parental Bereavement Pay.

You’ll need:

- the date of the death or stillbirth
- the date of the miscarriage if they’re employed in Northern Ireland
- the date your employee started working for you
- your employee’s salary details, including how often they’re paid
- the dates and number of weeks your employee wants to claim Statutory Parental Bereavement Pay
- your total Class 1 National Insurance contributions for the relevant year

[Check your employee’s eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/employers-parental-bereavement-pay-leave/eligibility) before using the calculator.
