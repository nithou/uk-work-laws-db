# Employment Tribunal

_Source: GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk/courts-tribunals/employment-tribunal — updated 2021-04-15_

We’re responsible for hearing claims from people who think someone such as an employer or potential employer has treated them unlawfully.

Examples of unlawful treatment claims that we may hear include:

- unfair dismissal
- discrimination
- unfair deductions from pay

## Who we are

We are an independent tribunal which makes decisions in legal disputes around employment law.
