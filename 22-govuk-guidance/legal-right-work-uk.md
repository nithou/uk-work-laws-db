# Check if a document allows someone to work in the UK

_Source: GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk/legal-right-work-uk — updated 2017-02-03_

Use this service to find out:

- which types of document give someone the right to work in the UK
- what a right to work check involves for each of them

Use a different service to [check a job applicant’s right to work](https://www.gov.uk/view-right-to-work) if they’ve given you their share code. You will not need to check their documents as well.
