# Employing people who are carers: advice and training

_Source: GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk/employers-for-carers — updated 2024-10-22_

Advice and training for employers with employees who are carers.

Employers for Carers   
[www.employersforcarers.org](http://www.employersforcarers.org)  
[employers@carersuk.org](mailto:employers@carersuk.org)   
Telephone: 020 7378 4956   
[Find out about call charges](https://www.gov.uk/call-charges)

You can get advice about:

- identifying carers in the workplace
- managing staff carer networks
- directing staff to other sources of support outside the workplace
- practical solutions to combining work and care

You can get training to help you with:

- employment law
- carers rights
- [flexible working](https://www.gov.uk/flexible-working)
- managing stress

Employers for Carers is a partner organisation of [Carers UK](http://www.carersuk.org/).
