# Plan your Shared Parental Leave and Pay

_Source: GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk/plan-shared-parental-leave-pay — updated 2021-06-17_

You can use this planning tool to find out:

- how and when you can take Shared Parental Leave alongside Maternity, Adoption and Paternity Leave
- how much Statutory Shared Parental Pay you’re entitled to while you take leave (your employer might offer more than the statutory amount)
- when you need to give notice to your employer

You can save your plan and edit it at any time.

You can choose to share the plan with other people (for example, your partner or employer) but it will not be shared with anyone automatically.

## Before you start

[Check if you and your partner are eligible for Shared Parental Leave or Pay](https://www.gov.uk/shared-parental-leave-and-pay). You can use the planning tool if at least one of you is eligible for either leave or pay.

You’ll also need:

- your child’s due date, birth date or placement date
- your salary
- your partner’s salary
