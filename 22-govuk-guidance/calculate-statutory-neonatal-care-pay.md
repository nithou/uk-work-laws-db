# Calculate your employee's Statutory Neonatal Care Pay

_Source: GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk/calculate-statutory-neonatal-care-pay — updated 2025-10-16_

Use this tool to calculate your employee’s Statutory Neonatal Care Pay.

You’ll need:

- the baby’s due date
- the baby’s date of birth (on or after 6 April 2025)
- your employee’s salary details, including how often they’re paid
- the number of consecutive [Neonatal Care Leave](https://www.gov.uk/neonatal-care-pay-leave/what-you-get) weeks being taken and the start date

[Check your employee’s eligibility](https://www.gov.uk/employers-neonatal-care-pay-leave/eligibility) before using the calculator.
