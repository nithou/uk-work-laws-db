# Guide for workplace representatives

There is no single works council equivalent to the French CSE. Representation in GB is layered:

| Body | Basis | Main role |
|---|---|---|
| **Recognised trade union** (voluntary or statutory recognition) | TULRCA Part I, Sch. A1 | Collective bargaining (at least pay, hours, holidays under statutory recognition), consultation on redundancies and TUPE, health and safety reps, information for bargaining |
| **Elected employee representatives** | TULRCA s.188A; TUPE reg. 14 | Consultation on collective redundancies and TUPE transfers when no recognised union |
| **Information and consultation (ICE) representatives** | ICE Regs 2004 (undertakings with 50+ employees) | Information and consultation on the business situation, employment and decisions likely to lead to substantial changes in work organisation or contracts |
| **Health and safety representatives** | Safety Representatives and Safety Committees Regs 1977 (union-appointed); H&S (Consultation with Employees) Regs 1996 (elected representatives of employee safety) | Consultation on health and safety, inspections, investigations, safety committees |
| **European Works Council** | TICE Regs 1999 (frozen since Brexit for existing EWCs) | Transnational information and consultation |
| Union learning reps, **equality reps** (new, ERA 2025) | TULRCA ss.168A, 168B | Learning and equality matters |

## 1. Trade union recognition and access (ERA 2025 changes)

- **Statutory recognition** (TULRCA Sch. A1) through the Central Arbitration Committee (CAC). From **6 April 2026**: the requirement that at least 40% of the bargaining unit vote in favour has been removed (simple majority of those voting), and the 10% membership test for admissibility can be lowered by regulations (first tranche of Sch. 6 ERA 2025). From **30 October 2026**: the remaining recognition reforms of ERA 2025 Sch. 6, including restrictions on unfair practices during the recognition process (check TULRCA Sch. A1 in `10-acts/` for the exact wording now in force).
- **Right of access to workplaces** for unions (new Part 4 of TULRCA via ERA 2025 s.59) — in force **30 October 2026**: unions may request access to meet, represent, recruit or organise workers (physical or digital); agreement or CAC determination; Code of Practice.
- **Statement of the right to join a union** in the written statement of particulars (ERA 2025 s.58) — expected January 2027.
- **Facilities for union representatives** (s.168(3A)) and **time off for equality representatives** (s.168B) — in force 30 October 2026.
- **Blacklisting** protections extended — expected April 2027.

## 2. Rights of union officials and representatives

| Right | Source | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Paid time off for **trade union duties** (officials of a recognised union) | TULRCA s.168, s.169 | Reasonable time off for negotiation, representation, consultation on redundancies/TUPE, and approved training; paid at normal rate; Acas Code on time off for trade union duties and activities |
| Accommodation and facilities | s.168(3A) (from 30 Oct 2026) | Reasonable accommodation and facilities when requested |
| Unpaid time off for **union activities** (members) | s.170 | Reasonable time off (not industrial action) |
| Union learning reps / equality reps | ss.168A, 168B | Paid time off for functions and training |
| Right to accompany | Employment Relations Act 1999 s.10 | Certified union officials can accompany at disciplinary and grievance hearings; paid time off to do so |
| Protection | TULRCA ss.146, 152; s.145A–B (inducements) | No detriment or dismissal for union membership or activities; offers to induce workers to leave the union or bargain individually are unlawful |
| Disclosure of information for collective bargaining | ss.181–185; Acas Code | Information without which the union would be materially impeded in bargaining and which good practice requires to disclose; complaint to the CAC |

## 3. Employee representatives (redundancy and TUPE)

- Election requirements (s.188A; TUPE reg. 14): fair election, enough representatives, candidates from affected employees, secret ballot, term long enough.
- Rights: reasonable paid time off to perform functions and for training (ERA ss.61–63), access to affected employees and accommodation and facilities, protection against detriment (s.47) and automatically unfair dismissal (s.103), as for candidates.
- Collective redundancy consultation and TUPE consultation: see `40-guides/redundancy.md` and `40-guides/contract-changes-fire-rehire-tupe.md`.
- **Protective award** for consultation failures doubled to **180 days' pay** (dismissals from 6 April 2026).

## 4. Information and consultation (ICE Regulations 2004)

- Undertakings with **50+ employees**. A valid employee request triggers negotiation of an agreement; the threshold for the request is **2% of employees (minimum 15)** — reduced from 10% from 6 April 2020.
- Standard provisions (if no agreement within 6 months): elected representatives (1 per 50 employees, between 2 and 25); information on recent and probable developments of activities and economic situation; information and consultation on the situation, structure and probable development of employment and threats to employment; information and consultation **with a view to reaching agreement** on decisions likely to lead to substantial changes in work organisation or contractual relations (reg. 20).
- Confidentiality (reg. 25) must be justified; complaints to the CAC; penalties up to £75,000 for failure to comply with a CAC declaration (EAT).
- Representatives: paid time off (reg. 27), protection against detriment and dismissal (regs. 30–32).

## 5. Health and safety representatives

- **Union safety reps** (1977 Regs): appointed by recognised unions; rights to investigate hazards and accidents, carry out inspections (every 3 months or after changes), receive information, be consulted in good time, request a **safety committee** (two or more reps request in writing — employer must set it up within 3 months), paid time off and training.
- **Representatives of employee safety** (1996 Regs): elected where there is no recognised union; consultation, information, time off.
- Protection against detriment and dismissal (ERA ss.44, 100) with no qualifying period and uncapped compensation for dismissal.
- Workers' right to leave and refuse to return to a place of serious and imminent danger (ERA ss.44(1)(d), 100(1)(d)).

## 6. Industrial action (key points as at November 2026)

- Immunity from tort requires a **trade dispute** (s.244), a valid **ballot** and proper **notices**.
- ERA 2025 changes in force from **18 February 2026** (s.159(2)): repeal of most of the Trade Union Act 2016 restrictions (ballot mandate extended from 6 to **12 months**; notice of industrial action to the employer reduced from 14 to **10 days**; simplified ballot and notice information requirements; removal of picket supervisor requirements; changes to political fund rules); repeal of the **Strikes (Minimum Service Levels) Act 2023** (18 December 2025).
- The **50% turnout** threshold for ballots remains until conditions for its removal are met (s.68, linked to electronic balloting).
- **Protection against dismissal** for protected industrial action strengthened from 18 February 2026 (s.77) and against **detriment** for taking part (30 October 2026).
- **Electronic and workplace balloting** permitted as means of voting from **25 August 2026** (Trade Unions (Permissible Means of Voting) and Employment Rights (Unfair Dismissal) (Amendment) Order 2026, SI 2026/881; new TULRCA s.226ZA).
- Support threshold: a simple majority of those voting (the extra 40% support requirement for important public services was removed on 18 February 2026, ERA 2025 s.69).

## 7. Practical toolkit for representatives

- Keep a log of requests, meetings, information received and refused.
- Request information in writing, citing the legal basis (s.181 TULRCA, s.188(4), TUPE reg. 13(2), ICE reg. 20).
- Challenge timing: consultation must start when proposals are formative and before decisions are made.
- Record the employer's responses to counter-proposals; consultation must be with a view to reaching agreement.
- Know the escalation routes: CAC (recognition, information disclosure, ICE, access), employment tribunal (time off, protective award, detriment), HSE (health and safety), Acas collective conciliation.
- Templates in `80-templates/`.
