# The Working Time Regulations 1998 — SCHEDULE 2 — WORKERS EMPLOYED IN AGRICULTURE in Wales or Scotland

_Revised text as published on legislation.gov.uk (version valid from 2026-10-01). Check the annotations and the list of amendments not yet applied below: some changes made by later legislation (notably the Employment Rights Act 2025) may not be reflected in the text yet._

### Paragraph 1
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1833/schedule/2/paragraph/1) · extent: E+W+S

Except where, in the case of a worker partly employed in agriculture in Wales or Scotland, different provision is made by a relevant agreement—
  (a) for the purposes of regulations 13, 13A and 15B, the leave year of a worker employed in agriculture in Wales or Scotland begins on 6th April each year or such other date as may be specified in an agricultural wages order which applies to him; and
  (b) the dates on which leave is taken by a worker employed in agriculture in Wales or Scotland shall be determined in accordance with an agricultural wages order which applies to him.

### Paragraph 2
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1833/schedule/2/paragraph/2)

Where, in the case referred to in paragraph 1 above, a relevant agreement makes provision different from sub-paragraph (a) or (b) of that paragraph—
  (a) neither section 11 of the Agricultural Wages Act 1948 nor section 11 of the Agricultural Wages (Scotland) Act 1949 shall apply to that provision; and
  (b) an employer giving effect to that provision shall not thereby be taken to have failed to comply with the requirements of an agricultural wages order.

### Paragraph 3
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1833/schedule/2/paragraph/3) · extent: E+W+S

In this Schedule, “an agricultural wages order" means an order under section 3 of the Agricultural Wages Act 1948 , section 3 of the Agricultural Sector (Wales) Act 2014 or section 3 of the Agricultural Wages (Scotland) Act 1949.
