# The Employment Tribunal Procedure Rules 2024 — Part 4 — Employer’s contract claim

_Revised text as published on legislation.gov.uk (version valid from 2026-03-02). Check the annotations and the list of amendments not yet applied below: some changes made by later legislation (notably the Employment Rights Act 2025) may not be reflected in the text yet._

### Rule 24 — Making an employer’s contract claim
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1155/rule/24) · extent: E+W+S

(1) A respondent who wishes to make an employer’s contract claim must do so by including it as part of the response to a claim which includes an employee’s contract claim, and the Tribunal must receive the response by the time specified in rule 17(1) (response), or by any extension of time granted under rule 21 (applications for extension of time for presenting response).
(2) The Tribunal may reject the whole or part of any employer’s contract claim on the ground that—
  (a) it has no jurisdiction, or
  (b) it does not contain any grounds on which the respondent relies, cannot sensibly be responded to or is an abuse of process.
(3) Where the Tribunal rejects an employer’s contract claim, rule 14 (reconsideration of rejection of claim) applies except that—
  (a) references to a claimant or a claim must be read as a reference to a respondent or to an employer’s contract claim respectively,
  (b) reference to rule 12 is to be read as a reference to rule 24, and
  (c) reference to rule 13 is to be ignored.

### Rule 25 — Notification of employer’s contract claim
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1155/rule/25) · extent: E+W+S

When the Tribunal sends a copy of the response form to the other parties in accordance with rule 23 (notification of acceptance) it must notify the claimant that the response includes an employer’s contract claim and include information on how the claimant may submit a reply, the time limit for doing so, and what will happen if the Tribunal does not receive a reply in time.

### Rule 26 — Replying to an employer’s contract claim
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1155/rule/26) · extent: E+W+S

(1) A claimant who wishes to reply to an employer’s contract claim must send their reply so that it is received by the Tribunal within 28 days of the date that a copy of the response form was sent to the claimant.
(2) If the Tribunal does not receive a reply by the end of the period specified in paragraph (1), rules 21 (applications for extension of time for presenting response) and 22 (effect of non-presentation or rejection of response, or case not contested) apply except that—
  (a) references to a respondent or a response must be read as a reference to a claimant or a reply, respectively,
  (b) references to a claimant must be read as a reference to a respondent, and
  (c) reference to rule 17(1) must be read as a reference to rule 26(1).
(2A) But the Tribunal may order a departure from rule 22 if—
  (a) the Tribunal does not receive a reply by the end of the period specified in paragraph (1), and
  (b) it considers it in the interests of justice to do so.
(3) Where the Tribunal accepts the reply it must send a copy of the reply to all other parties.
