### Paragraph 2
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/37/schedule/3A/paragraph/2) · extent: E+W+S

(1) This paragraph makes transitional modifications of the table as it applies to England and Wales.
(2) In relation to an offence committed before 2 May 2022, a reference to imprisonment for a term not exceeding ⟦still in force, to be replaced: 12 months⟧ the general limit in a magistrates’ court is to be read as a reference to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months.
(3) In relation to an offence committed before the commencement of section 281(5) of that Act (alteration of penalties for summary offences), a reference to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 51 weeks is to be read as a reference to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months.
