### Section 27C — ⟦partly in force / prospective: Qualifying tips, gratuities and service charges⟧
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/18/section/27C) · extent: E+W+S

(1) ⟦partly in force / prospective: This Part makes provision for—⟧

  (b) ⟦partly in force / prospective: the Secretary of State to issue a code of practice to promote fairness and transparency in relation to the distribution of qualifying tips, gratuities and service charges.⟧

(3) ⟦partly in force / prospective: An “employer-received tip” is an amount paid by a customer of an employer by way of a tip, gratuity or service charge (however described) which—⟧

  (a) ⟦partly in force / prospective: a worker-received tip is subject to employer control if the employer or an associated person exercises control or significant influence over the allocation of the tip;⟧
  (b) ⟦partly in force / prospective: a worker-received tip (“tip A”) is connected with another worker-received tip which is subject to employer control (“tip B”) if—⟧
    (i) ⟦partly in force / prospective: both tips are paid at, or are otherwise attributable to, the same place of business, and⟧
    (ii) ⟦partly in force / prospective: tip A is paid later in the week, or in the week following the week, in which tip B is paid.⟧
(6) ⟦partly in force / prospective: In this section the “⟧⟦partly in force / prospective: amount paid by a customer of an employer by way of a tip, gratuity or service charge⟧⟦partly in force / prospective: ” means the actual amount paid by the customer (and accordingly any deductions from the amount paid, whether deducted by the employer or any other person, are to be disregarded when determining the amount paid by the customer).⟧

(8) ⟦partly in force / prospective: If an amount is not paid in money, it can be a qualifying tip, gratuity or service charge only if it is paid in the form of a voucher, stamp, token or similar item which is—⟧
  (a) ⟦partly in force / prospective: of a fixed value expressed in monetary terms, and⟧
  (b) ⟦partly in force / prospective: capable of being exchanged (whether on its own or together with other vouchers, stamps, tokens or items, and whether immediately or only after a time) for money, goods or services (or for any combination of two or more of those things).⟧
(9) ⟦partly in force / prospective: In this section—⟧
- ⟦partly in force / prospective: “⟧⟦partly in force / prospective: payment arrangement⟧⟦partly in force / prospective: ” means an arrangement between an employer and another person under which payments made by customers of the employer are to be received by the other person instead of the employer;⟧
