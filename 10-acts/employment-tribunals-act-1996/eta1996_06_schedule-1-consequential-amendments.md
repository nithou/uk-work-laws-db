## The Transport Act 1968 (c.73)

### Paragraph 1
[legislation.gov.uk](http://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1996/17/schedule/1/paragraph/1) · extent: E+W+S

Section 135(4)(b) of the Transport Act 1968 shall continue to have effect with the substitution (originally made by paragraph 6 of Schedule 16 to the Employment Protection (Consolidation) Act 1978) of “ an employment tribunal ” for the words from “a tribunal" to the end.
