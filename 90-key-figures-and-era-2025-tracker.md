### How to use this tracker

1. Identify the **relevant date** (act, EDT, birth, ballot date).
2. Check whether the provision was in force at that date, and any transitional provisions in the commencement regulations (e.g. SI 2026/559 reg. 4 for unfair dismissal; SI 2026/954 reg. 4 for time limits).
3. In the revised texts, wording marked `⟦partly in force / prospective⟧` or `[PROSPECTIVE]` is not yet generally in force.
